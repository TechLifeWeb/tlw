About this version

Compact cards applies to Kanban only and is remembered per account in this browser. It hides notes, metadata, and the grouped priority/status selectors while keeping the title, age-based review badge, completion control, P1–P4 badge, and action icons. Edit opens all fields, including status.

Changes save to Todoist immediately. This page checks for updates every minute while visible and when you return. It pauses refresh while you edit.

Review dates stay in this browser, separated by Todoist account. No review control appears for the first 7 days. An amber “Review · Nd” badge appears after 7 days and turns red after 14 days. Click the badge to confirm “Still relevant” and restart the clock. Edits to titles, notes, schedules, or status here also restart it. Priority changes do not. Existing tasks start their review clock when first loaded.

In progress uses the label next_action_in_progress; Waiting uses next_action_waiting. Other labels are preserved. Recurring tasks advance to their next occurrence when completed.

Tokens and task text stay in page memory. Reloading requires connecting again. Disconnect clears the session, but retains your account’s review dates and view preferences.

Completion history from other sessions, project management, manual card ordering, email attachments, archive, and trash are not included yet.

Independent app. Not created by, affiliated with, or supported by Todoist.
