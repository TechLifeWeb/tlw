Nvidia Investor Lawsuit

An Nvidia shareholder filed a class-action lawsuit this week, accusing the chipmaker and its CEO, Jensen Huang, of misleading investors by failing to disclose his affair with a suspected Chinese spy.

Huang confirmed Sunday that he had an affair, which has been widely speculated to involve Singapore-based data center executive Alice Huang, whom a New York Times investigation suggested may be a Chinese spy (see previous write-up). The lawsuit alleges Nvidia misled shareholders by describing the pair's interactions as routine while failing to disclose potential national security concerns tied to its business with Alice Huang’s company, Megaspeed. A new report (free w/email) details how a top Nvidia executive intervened last year to halt a federal probe into whether Megaspeed was smuggling AI chips into China. Nvidia has reportedly sold Megaspeed more than $4.8B worth of chips over the past two years.

Nvidia shares fell another 5.5% yesterday, bringing the roughly $4T company's market value losses to more than $365B since Monday.

Impeachment Bid Blocked

The House voted 211-204 to shelve Rep. Thomas Massie’s (R, KY-4) articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. The move is expected to sideline the issue until Democrats take control of Congress next year.

Massie was the sole Republican to vote against referring the impeachment articles to committee. He had sought to impeach Hegseth on eight charges, several of which he characterized as violations of the War Powers Resolution of 1973. Many charges stemmed from the Iran war. One centered on the US campaign in Yemen, and another on strikes against alleged drug-smuggling boats in the Caribbean Sea and Pacific Ocean. A separate charge was tied to the US mission to capture Venezuela’s Nicolás Maduro. See the 34-page charging document here (scroll to “this link”).

Separately, House Republicans elected Rep. Jim Jordan (R, OH-4) to serve as minority leader in the next Congress. Senate Republicans reelected Sen. John Thune (R-SD) as party leader despite internal pushback over last week's electoral losses.

How Do You Set Up a Family Trust?

You've worked hard to build your savings, and you likely have a clear picture of who you'd like to benefit from it one day. For many investors, a family trust may be a key part of a smart estate plan, especially for preserving and passing on wealth.

A family trust is a specific type of trust you could use to help ensure your loved ones receive your assets according to your wishes. Assets held in the trust may also pass to your heirs without going through probate, which can help keep them out of the public record.

Historic Ship's Final Journey

The SS United States, once the world's fastest ocean liner, is scheduled to be sunk today off Florida's Gulf Coast, creating the world's largest artificial reef.

The 990-foot ship—roughly five city blocks long—will settle 180 feet underwater, about 22 nautical miles off Destin, where it could attract marine life and shelter fish (see how crews plan to sink it). Entering service in 1952, the vessel set a trans-Atlantic speed record on its maiden voyage, crossing the Atlantic in three days, 10 hours, and 40 minutes—a record that still stands for passenger liners. Designed by William Francis Gibbs with partial US military funding, the ship could be quickly converted to carry 14,000 troops, and its four 18-foot bronze propellers remained classified until the 1970s. Famous passengers included four US presidents, Marilyn Monroe, and Walt Disney. Retired in 1969, the vessel was purchased by Florida county officials for $1M in 2024.
