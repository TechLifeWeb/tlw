Hey there,

Quick heads-up from your 1440 team in case you're reading us on an Apple device.

Apple changed Mail. Some readers told us 1440 has started showing up in the 'Updates' tab with Apple’s new inbox categories and AI summaries. If you are using Apple Mail's new categories and we're landing somewhere besides 'Primary', do this once:

On iPhone/iPad (iOS 18.2+):

•

Open any 1440 email (even if it’s in Updates).

•

Tap ⋯ (top right) → Categorize Sender → choose Primary → Continue.

•

Done. Future 1440 issues go to Primary.

![](https://images.chamaileon.io/603e31c9224efc2a1990854e/66cc72b75ab7e9a5ec80078d/1754474372844_gif (1).gif)

On Mac (macOS Sequoia 15.4+)?

Open a 1440 email → Hover over our sender name ('1440 Daily Digest') and click the little drop-down arrow → Categorize Sender → Primary.

![](https://images.chamaileon.io/603e31c9224efc2a1990854e/66cc72b75ab7e9a5ec80078d/1754493510550_apple mail mac.gif)

Why we think it's worth your time: 1440 is designed to be quick, useful, and easy to scan, but only if you actually see it. Moving us to Primary helps make sure we land where you’ll notice us.

Thanks for all your support, and if you have any questions or thoughts, please reply to this email!

Take care,

The 1440 Team
