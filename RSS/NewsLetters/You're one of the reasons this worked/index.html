It started as a Google Doc sent to 78 people

Hey there,

Drew here, cofounder of 1440. No pitch in this one; just a quick story, then one small ask.

In May 2017, the first edition of 1440 was a Google Doc emailed to 78 friends and family. No publishing tools. No investors. No employees. I wrote it before dawn (starting at 4 AM) while working a full-time government job and raising a two-year-old. Tim had just left private equity and was betting his severance on the idea that people deserved a better morning read.

Today, 4.7 million people start their morning with 1440. Every word is still written and reviewed by humans. There's still no algorithm deciding what you see and no headline written to make you angry.

We got from 78 to 4.7 million the only way left once you refuse all of that: readers who liked it told someone.

Which is what makes you unusual. Out of those 4.7 million, you're in the small group who doesn't stop at the headline — you follow the story. You're the readers we write for first, the ones who actually want the full picture instead of the version that picks a side.

So, one ask. If you know someone who'd appreciate the news with facts and without the spin, pass 1440 along. It means more coming from you than from us.

[Share 1440]

Thank you for reading. It still means as much to us at 4.7 million as it did at 78.

— Drew
