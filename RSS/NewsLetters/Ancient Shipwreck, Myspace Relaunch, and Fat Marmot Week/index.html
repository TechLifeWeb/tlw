Irish Mob Boss Extradited

Daniel Kinahan, suspected head of one of the world’s largest drug syndicates, was taken into custody by Irish officials after his extradition from the United Arab Emirates yesterday. The transfer brings Kinahan home to face charges of directing organized crime and follows his April arrest in Dubai.

Formed in the 1990s to traffic street drugs in Dublin, the Kinahan network became one of Europe’s primary importers of illicit drugs, controlling an estimated one-third of the continent's cocaine supply and amassing more than $1B in net worth. The group’s rise was reportedly due to Kinahan’s coordination of a “supercartel” (podcast w/transcript) linking Central and South American cartels with other regional crime networks, including the Italian Mafia. His lavish 2017 wedding in Dubai, to which he had fled a year prior, was infiltrated by undercover DEA agents, allowing them to begin mapping the worldwide network.

Roman Shipwreck Revealed

A Roman-era shipwreck was discovered off the coast of Sicily, Italy’s culture minister announced Saturday. The ship measures about 20 feet wide and 70 feet long and was discovered alongside hundreds of well-preserved clay jars. Scroll for dive footage.

The discovery came after a tipoff from a local fisherman, who found what appeared to be a rock while freediving three miles off the coast of Mazara del Vallo. He dove down and discovered the ship before sharing the finding with local authorities. At a depth of about 150 feet, cultural heritage divers from Palermo police confirmed the discovery of the ancient ship and jars, identifying them as the long cylindrical jars typically used to carry wine, olive oil, or grain. Italian authorities will now safeguard and investigate the site to learn more about its history.

Myspace Nostalgia Pitch

Myspace’s owners revealed in a yet-to-be publicly available documentary that they seek to relaunch the social media site. Owners Tim and Chris Vanderhook have not announced a date for relaunch as of this writing.

In the early 2000s, the site, then stylized as MySpace, became the world’s most popular social media platform. The company boasted 115 million monthly visitors by 2008; however, it was soon surpassed by Facebook. Despite a series of rebrands and ownership transfers in the 2010s, including one involving singer Justin Timberlake, the company never regained its status as a meaningful social media competitor.

The owners’ plans come amid a broader cultural backlash against social media sites like Meta and TikTok, which have been criticized for addictive algorithms and correlations with poor mental health, including in children (see latest Meta penalty).
