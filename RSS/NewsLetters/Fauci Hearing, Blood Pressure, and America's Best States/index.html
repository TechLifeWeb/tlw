Fauci Invokes the Fifth

Dr. Anthony Fauci declined to answer over 100 questions during a Senate committee hearing yesterday on COVID-19's origins, citing concerns that Republicans could use his testimony against him.

Fauci, who served as the nation's infectious disease expert during the pandemic, maintains the virus likely spilled naturally from animals to humans. This view aligns with a 3.5-year independent assessment that says COVID-19 likely emerged from animal-to-human transmission but could not conclusively determine its origins. Homeland Security Chairman Sen. Rand Paul (R-Ky.) aligns with the theory that COVID-19 emerged from a US-funded lab in China. Ahead of yesterday's hearing, Paul released 1,141 pages of Fauci's personal diary, which show the doctor expressing some doubts about a natural origin. See more diary takeaways here.

Paul said he will schedule a vote next week on whether to hold Fauci in contempt for invoking the Fifth Amendment. The senator said the move was unwarranted since a Biden-era preemptive pardon shields Fauci from some criminal liability.

Saudi Arabia Steps In

Saudi Arabia confirmed yesterday it publicly joined the US' military campaign against Iran. The kingdom participated in a joint strike on Iranian-backed militias in Iraq, reportedly killing at least 20 combatants.

In announcing the strikes, the kingdom's Ministry of Defense cited Iranian attacks on its oil industry: Saudi Arabia blames Iraqi militias for up to half of the nearly 1,000 drone strikes it has weathered since February. Earlier this week, drones and missiles struck Saudi Arabia's Abqaiq oil facility (see map). Although Riyadh had not openly participated in military offensive action until this week, US officials say the kingdom has secretly participated in US-led attacks in the past.

Blood Pressure Boom

High blood pressure among pregnant women in the US rose 73% between 2016 and 2024, according to government data released yesterday. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention reported one in 10 pregnant women had high blood pressure in 2024, up from one in 17 in 2016.

Hypertensive disorders—including gestational hypertension and preeclampsia—are among the leading causes of pregnancy-related death and illness in the US. They can increase the risk of preterm birth, low birth weight, stillbirth, placental complications, and organ failure in mothers. Women who develop high blood pressure during pregnancy also face a greater lifetime risk of heart disease.

The new report is based on hospital-collected birth certificate data and includes only high blood pressure that develops during pregnancy. Blood pressure measures the force of blood pushing against artery walls. It rises when the heart pumps harder, blood vessels narrow or stiffen, or the body retains more fluid. 1440 Civics: The Gilded Age

The Gilded Age, a term popularized by Mark Twain's novel of the same name, was a period from the 1870s to 1900. The era was defined by rapid industrialization, wealth inequality, political corruption, and immigration into America's fast-growing cities.
