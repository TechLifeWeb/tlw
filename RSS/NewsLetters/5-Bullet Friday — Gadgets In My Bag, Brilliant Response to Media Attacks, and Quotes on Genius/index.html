5-Bullet Friday #

The latest from author and investor Tim Ferriss

Hi All!

Here is your weekly dose of 5-Bullet Friday, a list of what I’m pondering and exploring.

This edition is brought to you by ​The Way, the meditation app I use most often. Be sure to see their offer in the P.S.

What I’m listening to #

“Inevitable Ending” (Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music), by Armen Miran and Hraach (Viken Arman and Goldcap remix). Great music for a million things. I’m currently using it for writing sessions, but it’s versatile.

What I’m reading #

​An open letter from the French businessman who runs LVMH Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton, the world’s largest luxury goods company. “Bernard Arnault rarely responds to criticism in public, but this time he published a detailed open letter after Le Monde spent 6 months investigating him and LVMH.”
​
The above is a case study in clever media-crisis management.

Tool that’s always on me #

​ProBody Pilates Ball. Yep, it’s a Pilates ball, but I don’t use it for Pilates. I keep it deflated in my backpack, and if I need lumbar support or butt padding anywhere on the road (in hotels, restaurants, airplanes, etc.), I give it literally 2–3 puffs of partial inflation and voilà! Deflated, this is small enough to fold up and stick in a jacket or jeans pocket.

Another tiny gadget in my backpack #

​Sennheiser Pro Audio Condenser Microphone. This lav mic plugs straight into an iPhone and makes on-the-go recordings sound much better than any built-in laptop or phone mic. It’s remarkable what quality you can get, especially if paired with the Ferrite app that allows you to record high-fidelity audio straight on your iPhone.

Quote I’m pondering #

“Most geniuses—especially those who lead others—prosper not by deconstructing intricate complexities but by exploiting unrecognized simplicities.”
​― Andy Benoit, “THE CASE FOR THE... BRONCOS”

You can complement this edition of 5-Bullet Friday with the latest podcast episode of the "How to Simplify Your Life" series, featuring advice from Chip Conley, Greg McKeown, David Allen, Elizabeth Gilbert, and Oliver Burkeman.

Have a wonderful weekend, all.

Much love to you and yours,
​
Tim
