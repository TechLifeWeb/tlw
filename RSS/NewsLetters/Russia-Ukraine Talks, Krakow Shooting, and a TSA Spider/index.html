Ukraine-Russia Negotiations Resume

Representatives from the US, Ukraine, and Russia will meet in Abu Dhabi tomorrow for a new round of talks. The two-day meeting is the fourth round of US-brokered trilateral negotiations organized by the Trump administration and the first since February.

President Donald Trump predicted an agreement could be produced soon, potentially involving all of Europe, after meeting with a Russian envoy at the White House last week. Special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner are expected to participate in the US delegation, with Secretary of State Marco Rubio suggesting progress has already been made on what postwar security infrastructure could look like. France said yesterday Europe will be represented, although it is not clear to what extent it will be involved in negotiations.

Kyiv and Moscow continued engaging in attacks over the weekend, including through drone strikes and ballistic missiles. See the latest war updates here.

Krakow Shooting

Polish prosecutors opened a terrorism investigation yesterday into a mass shooting in Krakow’s main market square that killed at least six people and wounded 15 others. It marks Poland’s deadliest shooting since the post-communism era began in 1989.

Around 3 pm local time Saturday, a 24-year-old Polish man opened fire from his car by Rynek Główny, a historic plaza that attracts more than 40,000 visitors daily. He used an automatic weapon to shoot randomly (see video—warning, sensitive). Police later searched the suspect’s home about 93 miles northeast of Krakow and found the bodies of his father, mother, and younger sister. The gunman is in critical condition as of this writing after being shot by police. Authorities described him as having ultranationalistic views.

Rockefeller Tree Arrives

The Rockefeller Christmas tree arrived in New York City Saturday, kicking off a tourist season that will see up to an estimated 750,000 people visit daily.

The tradition dates back to 1931, when construction workers at what would become Rockefeller Plaza pooled resources to buy a 20-foot balsam fir tree (see more via YouTube). Since then, the tradition has involved larger trees, typically a Norway spruce lit with more than 50,000 LED lights and topped with a roughly 900-pound Swarovski-crystal star. This year’s tree is a 76-foot-tall, 11-ton Norway spruce from West Edmeston, New York, planted around 75 years ago. The tree will be lit from Dec. 2 to Jan. 9, after which the wood will be donated to Habitat for Humanity.
