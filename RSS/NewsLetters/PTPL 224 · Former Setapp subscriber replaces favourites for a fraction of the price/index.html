Bye-bye, big library of rented apps

My Setapp subscription ran out last week; I am officially a former Setapp subscriber. After 6 years of happily paying one fee for access to more than 200 apps, it's time for a break.

Not because I'm unhappy with the value, but because right now I need a little less in my life. Less choices, less distraction, less bills. I still heartily recommend Setapp to Mac users who want a cost effective way to try out new apps. Or who might need a way, as I did, to short circuit their app addiction.

Setapp is amazingly good value for the money! If it sounds like a good fit for where you are at and you’d like to try it for a full 30 days rather than the standard 7, you can see if my referral link (use the code ELLANE) is still working. I wouldn’t say no to a commission that comes at no extra cost to you. Otherwise seek out someone else’s referral and join via that.

Here's the full list of apps I had installed before my Setapp subscription expired. Apps in bold are those I've bought outright or replaced. The rest are ones I wasn't using at that time.

  • Antinote
  • CleanMyMac (Mole)
  • Cleanshot (Shottr)
  • Downie
  • Due (Reminders)
  • Hookmark (Hookmark free)
  • Image2icon (Flairy)
  • Marked
  • MindNode Classic
  • Numi
  • One Switch
  • Paste (Raycast)
  • PDF Squeezer (PDF Squeezer)
  • PopChar
  • Popclip (Popclip)
  • SideNotes
  • Squash (Squoosh)
  • Taskpaper
  • TextSniper (Shottr)
  • Typeface (FontBase)
