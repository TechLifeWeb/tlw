How to Let Go Tour #

Joshua Fields Millburn

What becomes possible when we stop clinging?

Last year, I started writing a book called How to Let Go. But the more I wrote, the more I realized something wasn't working. The ideas made sense on my screen, but they really came alive on stage—in the laughter, the pauses, the tears, and the interactions with the audience.

Turns out, some ideas deserve a stage more than a page. So I closed the book and started building my first solo live show.

Today, I'm excited to announce the first three cities:

Seattle — Sep 11 (tickets)

Vancouver — Sep 12 (tickets)

New York City — Oct 18 (tickets)

At these events, we’ll explore the heavy burdens we carry: clutter, expectations, anxiety, resentment, and everything else that weighs us down. Through funny personal stories and serious practical ideas, we'll discover what happens when we finally let go.

I can't wait to see you there.

—JFM
