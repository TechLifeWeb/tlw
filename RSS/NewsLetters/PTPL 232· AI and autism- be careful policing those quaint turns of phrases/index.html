Preview (read the full 2+ minute post for more details)
This week I take a look at accessibility issues and solutions for people with one or more disabilities. Things aren’t always as they seem, for bad and for good! Some autistic people naturally write like AI — whoops! I mean, AI has learned to write using patterns that come naturally to some autistic people. And some non-autistic people, for that matter.

---

“More Than One Way In” is a succinct, 6-module course designed to help people who have multiple disabilities to discover that they can learn, and different ways to do it. It’s also for anyone who has ever felt that the learning methods they’ve experienced were not right for them. Each module only takes a few minutes. I finished the whole thing in one sitting and loved it!
