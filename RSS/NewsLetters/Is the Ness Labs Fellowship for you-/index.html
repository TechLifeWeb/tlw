Is the Ness Labs Fellowship for you? #

Hi friends,

Last week, I introduced the new Ness Labs Fellowship, and I wanted to share a little more about who it’s for and how it will work.

The Fellowship is for people who want a more guided, hands-on way to practice metacognition and run experiments with regular live support along the way.

Each month, we’ll meet twice:

  • One Curiosity Quest session, where we’ll practice metacognition and pay attention to the patterns and possibilities worth exploring
  • One Experimental Coaching session, where we’ll turn those insights into tiny experiments, learn from what happens, and decide what to try next

Between sessions, you’ll have access to a private mastermind with other Fellows, so you can share what you’re trying and what you’re learning along the way.

The Fellowship is $249/year and is for those who want more live guidance and support to develop their experimental practice, whether that’s on your own or with others (colleagues, team, family).

And if you don’t need that level of support (and despite the advice from many of my entrepreneur friends telling me to raise my prices!), the regular Membership is still there for you at $49/year, with the same self-paced courses and peer-learning sessions.

All sessions will be recorded, so you don’t need to worry if you can’t make every single one live.

I’ve also brought someone new onto the Ness Labs team to help me run the Fellowship. Many of you already know Remy from the Personal Knowledge Management sessions she’s been leading over the past year, and I’m excited to work with her to design the best experience possible.

We’ll kick things off with our first live call on October 1st.
